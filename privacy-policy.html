2. Information We Collect and Store

A. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

HexaGO does not require user registration, accounts, or personal profiles. We do not collect, process, or store:

  • Real names, physical addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Financial or banking information.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

HexaGO functions as a self-contained logic puzzle application. The following gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device storage (via localStorage / application sandboxed storage):

  • In-game currency balances (coins and gems earned through puzzles).
  • Daily challenge records and streak counters.
  • Sound, music, ambient audio, and visual theme settings.

This information is never transmitted to any private developer server and remains under your local control.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

How to Delete Your Local Game Data

You can permanently delete all local game data at any time through your device settings:

  1. Open your Android Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps > HexaGO > Storage.
  3. Tap Clear Data (and Clear Cache).

Alternatively, uninstalling HexaGO from your device immediately removes all local storage files.

How to Manage, Reset, or Delete Your Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android settings:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Go to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  4. Optionally toggle on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Contact for Data Requests

Because we do not operate a remote user account database, we do not store your personal information on our own servers. For any data inquiries or confirmations, you may email us directly at studioskibrit@gmail.com.
