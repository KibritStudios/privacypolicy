## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not require account registration, login, email addresses, phone numbers, real names, or physical addresses.
- **No Sensitive Data:** We do not collect biometric data, financial credentials, health data, or contact lists.
- **No Precise Geolocation:** We do not request or track GPS or precise location data.

### B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
HexaGO stores gameplay and configuration data locally on your device storage (via `localStorage` and app preferences):
- Game level progression, campaign unlocks, and scores.
- In-game currencies (coins and gems earned or spent).
- Daily challenge completion records and streak counters.
- Audio preferences (music, sound effects, ambient toggles).
- Unlocked visual cosmetics and tile styles.

**This gameplay data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to private developer servers.**

### C. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties
HexaGO integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to provide advertisement delivery and platform functionality. These third-party services may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device-identifying information:
- **Device Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address, device model, operating system version, and system language.
- **Usage & Diagnostic Metrics:** Ad interactions, impressions, click rates, crash logs, and performance metrics.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

### How to Delete Local Gameplay Data
All local game progression, coins, gems, and settings can be permanently deleted at any time:
1. **Via Android Settings:** Go to `Settings > Apps > HexaGO > Storage > Clear Data` (or `Clear Cache`).
2. **Via Uninstallation:** Uninstalling HexaGO from your device immediately removes all local storage files.

### How to Reset or Delete Advertising Identifiers
You have full control over the advertising identifier processed by Google AdMob on your Android device:
1. Open your Android device **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google > Ads** (or **Privacy > Ads** depending on your Android version).
3. Tap **"Delete advertising ID"** or **"Reset advertising ID"**.
4. You may also enable **"Opt out of Ads Personalization"**.

### Data Subject Deletion Requests
Because HexaGO does not maintain an online account system or central database of user profiles, we do not store your personal information on our own servers. If you have any questions or wish to confirm data deletion, you may contact us at [studioskibrit@gmail.com](mailto:studioskibrit@gmail.com).
